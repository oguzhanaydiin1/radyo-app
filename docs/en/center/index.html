Install one app.
Reach them all.

No more hunting for each app or keeping track of updates. OA Labs Center knows them all and tells you when a new version is out.

  1. Install OA Labs CenterOn Windows or your Android phone. It finds the OA Labs apps you already have.
  2. Install any app in one clickDiscover lists them all: Pusula Browser, Pusula Köprü, OA Radyo, Terazi, Harrier Cleaner. For upcoming ones, “Notify me”.
  3. Updates take care of themselvesYou get a notification when a new version is out; “Update all” does them in one go. On Windows it can install them by itself.

What’s inside?

Home base for OA Labs apps: installs, updates, your account and support in one place.

Install and update

  • Home: your installed apps and their versions; “Open” and “Update”
  • Update all: pending updates in one place
  • App page: features, screenshots, version history; Install, Open, Uninstall

Account and devices

  • Single sign-in: sign in to Center and Pusula opens signed in
  • My devices: apps on your phone and PC side by side
  • Remote install: install an app on your PC from your phone (or the other way round); the PC installs it itself, the phone asks you to confirm
  • No password: sign in with a code sent to your email

News and support

  • What’s new: release notes of every OA app in one feed
  • Announcements: important news from OA Labs on the home screen and as a notification
  • Support: feedback form, FAQ, ideas board, roadmap
  • Runs in the background on Windows: sits by the clock and checks for updates every 6 hours

Two-minute setup

Windows

  1. Use “Download for Windows” to get OA-Labs-Center-Kurulum.exe and open it. If Windows warns you, choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. No admin rights needed.
  2. OA Labs Center shows your installed apps; install anything else from Discover.
  3. Sign in with your OA Labs account under “Account and settings” if you like; that turns on your devices and single sign-in.

Android

  1. Open this page on your phone and tap “Download for Android”. Open the downloaded file; if asked, turn on “Allow from this source” and tap “Install”.
  2. Open OA Labs Center. On its first install, Android asks to “Allow from this source” for Center too; turn it on.
  3. Allow notifications for updates and announcements.

Questions

Do I need an account?

Not to install, open or update apps. For My devices, remote installs and single sign-in you sign in with your OA Labs account; there’s no password, you sign in with a code sent to your email.

How do remote installs work?

Both devices need to be signed in to the same account. Tap “Install on PC” on your phone and, if OA Labs Center is running on the PC, it downloads and installs the app by itself. Installing from the PC to the phone needs one “Install” tap on the phone, as Android requires.

What is single sign-in?

When you sign in to OA Labs Center, Pusula Browser on Windows opens signed in too; on the phone just tap “Sign in with OA Labs Center” in Pusula. If you signed in to Pusula first, Center opens signed in.

Does it run in the background?

On Windows, closing the window sends it to the icon by the clock; it starts with Windows and checks for updates every 6 hours. You can turn this off under “Account and settings”. On Android it checks for updates and announcements once a day.

If I uninstall Center, are the apps removed?

No. Each app is installed on its own; removing OA Labs Center doesn’t touch them.

Which apps are there?

Pusula Browser, Pusula Köprü, OA Radyo, Terazi and Harrier Cleaner. New apps are added to the list as they come out, without updating Center.

Why does Windows show a warning?

The app isn’t digitally signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” on first launch. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Does it cost anything? Any ads?

No. OA Labs Center is completely free and ad-free.
