About

OA Labs is Oğuzhan Aydın’s software workshop. Small apps, carefully made.

What we make

Apps that make things you use every day simpler, faster and more respectful:

  • Pusula Browser: a fast, ad-free browser for Windows and Android that syncs between your devices.
  • OA Radyo: radio stations from Turkey and around the world in one app.
  • Harrier Cleaner: a cleanup tool that scans and cleans your computer on the computer itself and never sends your files anywhere.
  • On the way: OA Score (live scores and match news) and OA Barkod.

How we make them

  • Free. All our apps are free, with no subscriptions or hidden costs.
  • No ads. We don’t show ads and don’t use your data for advertising.
  • Privacy first. Most of your data stays on your own device. Our privacy policy says plainly what goes where.
  • No account required. The apps work without an account; an OA Labs account is only for optional features such as syncing between devices.
  • Frequent updates. We fix reported bugs quickly; every release is described in What’s new.

Get in touch

For questions, suggestions or bug reports, use the feedback page or write to destek@oalabs.dev. See what’s planned on the roadmap.
