Enter it once.
Terazi remembers the rest.

No more typing your loan instalment, rent or subscription every month. Add it once, choose how many months it runs, and Terazi asks on the payment day.

  1. Add income and spendingType the amount, pick a category. Terazi works out what’s left this month and how much you can spend per day until the month ends.
  2. Add your regular paymentsLoan, rent, internet, bills… Choose the day of the month and how many months it runs; for a loan already under way, enter the number of the next instalment.
  3. “Paid yet?” on the dayYou get a notification on your phone. Tap “Paid” and it’s recorded as spending; or choose “Remind me tomorrow” or “Not this month”.

What’s inside?

Simple and quick: adding a purchase takes two taps. Everything runs on your device.

Every day

  • Left this month and what you can spend per day until month end, with upcoming payments already deducted
  • Quick entry: keypad, category, date and a note
  • Activity: month by month, day by day; search and income/spending filter
  • Fix or delete a wrong entry; tapped “Paid” by mistake? Undo it

Payment calendar

  • Regular payments: add once and they land in the calendar every month
  • How many months? 12, 24, 36 or ongoing; a loan shows as “instalment 8 of 24”
  • “Paid yet?” prompt and Android notification; optionally a reminder 1 or 3 days before
  • Amounts that vary: like bills; on the day it asks for the real amount

Document vault

  • Bills, insurance, warranties, contracts: scan with your phone’s camera or add a PDF/photo
  • Expiry reminders: reminds you 30, 15 or 7 days before a policy or warranty ends
  • Linked to payments: attach the bill when you tap “Paid”
  • On your device only, encrypted: documents never go to a server; you keep them with a password-protected backup file

Budgets and reports

  • Category budgets: groceries, fun… set a monthly limit; it turns red when you go over
  • Reports: spending over the last six months and a breakdown by category
  • Compared with last month, and the category that grew the most
  • Export on Windows: all activity as CSV, opens in Excel
  • Turkish and English, light and dark theme; lira, dollar, euro, pound, rouble, manat and franc

Two-minute setup

Windows

  1. Use “Download for Windows” to get Terazi-Kurulum.exe and open it. If Windows warns you, choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. No admin rights needed.
  2. Open Terazi and add your first income.
  3. Press Ctrl+N any time for a quick new entry.

Android

  1. Open this page on your phone and tap “Download for Android”. Open the downloaded file; if asked, turn on “Allow from this source” and tap “Install”.
  2. Open Terazi; when you add your first regular payment it asks for notification permission, tap “Allow”.
  3. Notifications arrive at 9 a.m. on the payment day.

Questions

Where is my data kept?

Only on your device, in the app’s own folder. Terazi needs no account; your entries, amounts, payments and documents are never sent to a server. Documents are stored encrypted. Uninstalling the app deletes everything, so make a password-protected backup now and then under Settings → Backup and transfer. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I store my ID card or bank card?

No, Terazi isn’t meant for them. There’s no ID type, and you’ll see a warning when adding documents: a photo of an ID or card on a lost phone could be misused.

How do I back up, and what if I change phones?

Settings → Backup and transfer → “Create encrypted backup”. Your entries and documents go into one file encrypted with a password you choose; save it to Google Drive or your computer. On the new device, “Restore from backup” brings everything back. If you forget the password, the backup can’t be opened, not even by us.

Do my phone and PC sync?

Not yet: each device keeps its own entries. We’re working on end-to-end encrypted sync with your OA Labs account; this page will say when it’s out.

My loan is already under way. What do I do?

When adding the regular payment, choose the total number of instalments (e.g. 24) and which instalment the next payment is (e.g. 8). Terazi works out the end date; months before you added it count as paid.

What about bills whose amount changes?

Turn on “Amount may vary” when adding the payment. You enter a rough amount; on the day Terazi asks for the real one and records that.

I don’t get notifications

On Android 13 and later Terazi needs notification permission (Settings → Apps → Terazi → Notifications). Some phones stop background apps to save battery; removing Terazi’s battery restriction helps. Notifications arrive at 9 a.m. on the payment day.

Can I export to Excel?

On Windows, “Export” saves all activity to your Downloads folder as a CSV file that opens straight in Excel. Not on Android yet.

Why “Beta”?

Terazi is new. The core features are ready and in use, and it’s improving fast with feedback. If you spot a bug, tell us on the feedback page.

Why does Windows show a warning?

The app isn’t digitally signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” on first launch. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

How does it update?

The easiest way is OA Labs Center: it tells you about new versions and installs them in one click. The Android version is always signed with the same OA key, so new versions install over the old one and your entries stay.

Does it cost anything? Any ads?

No. Terazi is completely free and ad-free.
