What’s inside?

Everything runs on your computer; your files are never sent anywhere. Deleted items go to the Recycle Bin first.

Free up space

  • Smart Scan: runs every module in one go and gives you a short summary
  • Junk Files: temporary files, browser caches, error reports, Windows Update leftovers, the Recycle Bin
  • Duplicate Files: compares by content, so it finds copies even with different names
  • Disk Map: the folders taking the most space; pick any folder and drill down

Speed up and check

  • Startup Apps: the same sources as Task Manager; turn them on or off with one click
  • Uninstaller: runs the program’s own uninstaller, then clears the leftover folder
  • System Info: live CPU, memory, graphics, disk and network use
  • Battery & Health: battery capacity and cycle count, disk health

Photos and privacy

  • Photo Compressor: shrinks JPEG photos; originals are backed up to the Recycle Bin
  • Photo Location Remover: removes the location record from photos without touching the image
  • Screenshots: swipe through piled-up screenshots and delete the ones you don’t need Soon

Installs in a minute

Windows

  1. Use “Download for Windows” to get the installer.
  2. Open it. If Windows shows a warning, choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. No administrator rights needed.
  3. When it’s done, open Harrier Cleaner and start a Smart Scan.

Questions

Could something be deleted by mistake?

No. Harrier Cleaner never deletes anything without your approval. Duplicate files, leftovers of removed programs and the originals of compressed photos go to the Recycle Bin first, so you can restore them if you change your mind.

Are my files sent anywhere?

No. Scanning and cleaning happen entirely on your computer; your file names, contents and photos are never sent anywhere. Details are in the privacy policy.

Why does Windows show a warning?

The app isn’t digitally signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” the first time. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Why does it ask for administrator rights for some info?

Windows only shares some hardware details, such as disk temperature and wear, with administrator rights. If needed, you can restart the app as administrator from Settings; everything else works without it.

Is it paid? Are there ads?

No. Harrier Cleaner is completely free and ad-free.

When is the Android version coming?

It’s in the works. To hear when it’s out, press “Notify me” at the top of this page; you’ll get a single email.

How does it update?

When it starts, the app checks for a new version; if there is one, it lets you know and installs it in one click.
