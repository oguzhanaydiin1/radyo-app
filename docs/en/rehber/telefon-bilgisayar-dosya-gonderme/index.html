Sending files and notes from your phone to your computer

To get a photo from your phone onto your computer, most of us message ourselves on WhatsApp. There’s a shorter way.

OA Flow in Pusula Browser

OA Flow is a chat-like box between your phone and computer, signed in with the same OA Labs account. Drop notes, links, photos and small files into it, and the other device picks them up.

One-time setup

  1. Install Pusula Browser on both your computer and your phone.
  2. On both devices, tap the account icon (top right on your computer, top left of the start page on your phone), enter your email and type the code you receive. No password; membership is free.

Sending

  1. Open OA Flow from the menu.
  2. Type a note or link, or add a photo or file with the paperclip icon (up to 600 KB).
  3. Send. The other device receives it when you open the app, or within a few minutes while it’s open; received files go to Downloads.

Privacy: what you send waits on our server until the receiving device picks it up, and is then deleted from the server. Details are in our privacy policy.

Sending the open page to your other device

To finish on your computer something you started reading on your phone, use menu → Send to other device. Or do nothing: if you’re signed in, your phone’s open tabs already appear on your computer under menu → Tabs on other devices.

If you don’t want an account, choose menu → This page → Send to phone with a QR code on your computer and scan it with your phone’s camera.

For large files

For files bigger than 600 KB (videos, long PDFs), a cloud folder such as Google Drive or OneDrive, or Windows’ “Nearby sharing”, works better.

Pusula Browser is free, for Windows and Android.
