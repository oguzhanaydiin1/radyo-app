Watching YouTube without ads

Ads before videos, ad boxes on the page, sponsored shopping suggestions… There are two ways to get rid of them.

1. The official way: YouTube Premium

YouTube’s own paid subscription. It removes ads on every device and also unlocks background play and downloads on your phone. If you watch a lot of YouTube and want to support creators, it’s the most solid option.

2. An ad-blocking browser

If you’d rather not pay, you can open YouTube in a browser that blocks ads. In Chrome you need an extension, and after recent changes to Chrome’s extension rules some of them don’t work as well as they used to.

In Pusula Browser the ad blocker is built in and on by default:

  • If a video ad starts, it’s muted and skipped to the end right away, and the “Skip ad” button is pressed for you.
  • Ads and “Sponsored” shopping boxes on the home page, in search results and in the side column of the watch page are hidden.
  • More than 3,600 ad and tracking servers are blocked, so pages load faster too.

Step by step in Pusula

  1. Download and install Pusula Browser (buttons below).
  2. Open youtube.com. There’s nothing else to turn on; if the shield to the right of the address bar is green, blocking is on.
  3. If something doesn’t work right on a site, click the shield to turn blocking off for that site only.

Tip: To work in another tab while you watch, use menu → This page → “Watch video in a small window”; the video plays in the corner of the screen, always on top. If a background tab is playing sound, click the speaker icon on the tab to mute it.

YouTube changes how it shows ads often, so an ad may sometimes flash for a moment. If you notice it, let us know on the feedback page and we’ll fix it quickly.

Pusula Browser is free, for Windows and Android.
