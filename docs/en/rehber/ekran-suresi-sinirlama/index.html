Limiting the time you spend on a site

If “I’ll just look for five minutes” turns into hours, seeing how much time actually passes is a good first step.

How much time am I spending?

In Pusula Browser, open menu → Screen time. You’ll see how long you spent in the browser today and this week, charts by day and hour, and the sites you spent the most time on. Screen time is tracked automatically; there’s nothing to turn on.

Only time you actually spend counts: if you don’t touch anything for a while, counting pauses, but it continues while you watch a video.

Setting a daily limit for a site

  1. On the Screen time screen, tap the site in the list (for example youtube.com).
  2. Pick a time under Daily limit: 15 min, 30 min, 45 min, 1 h, 1.5 h, 2 h or 3 h.
  3. To block the site for the day instead of only getting a warning when time is up, turn on Lock the site when time is up.

When you open a locked site, a page tells you the limit is reached. If you really need it, choose 15 more minutes; the limit resets the next day.

Daily goal and weekly summary

  • Daily goal: choose how long you want to spend in the browser each day in total; you’re told when you’re close and when you reach it.
  • Weekly summary: on Android, a “This week you spent …” notification arrives every Sunday at 8 pm.
  • Merge devices: if you’re signed in, your phone’s and computer’s time can be shown together (optional).

Privacy: screen time data is kept and calculated on your device and isn’t sent anywhere unless you turn on “Merge devices”.

Pusula Browser is free, for Windows and Android.
