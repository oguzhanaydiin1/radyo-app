Sync

One account.
Two devices. Always the same.

Sign in with your OA Labs account on your PC and your phone; Pusula Browser keeps them in sync on its own. The page you were reading on your phone is one tap away on your PC, and a password saved on one is ready on the other.

  • BookmarksA page you star on your PC is in your phone’s bookmarks too.
  • Quick links (Tiles)The sites on your start page, in the same order, on both devices.
  • Open tabsYour phone’s tabs show up on your PC under “Tabs on other devices”, and the other way round. Send a tab to the other device in one tap.
  • OA FlowSend notes, links, photos and small files between your phone and PC; it flows like a chat, and received files land in Downloads.
  • HistoryBoth devices share one history; delete something on one and it’s gone from the other. Private tabs are never saved.
  • Passwords, end-to-end encryptedYour passwords leave the device encrypted with your own sync passphrase; nobody, OA Labs included, can read them.
  • OA Labs sign-inSign in in the app and you’re signed in on oalabs.dev too; sign in on the site and the app knows.

  1. Open Pusula Browser on both devices and tap the account icon (top right on your PC, top left of the start page on your phone).
  2. Type your email and enter the code you get. No password, and the account is free.

You don’t need an account; every feature works without signing in. You choose what syncs under “Sync settings” in the menu.

What’s inside?

The things you use every day, in a clean interface. Anything without a Windows or Android tag is on both.

Ad-free and safe

  • Ad and tracker blocking: more than 3,600 ad and tracking servers are blocked and the gaps ads leave behind are closed; turn it off per site if you like
  • Ad-free YouTube: video ads are skipped, and ad and sponsored shopping boxes on the page are hidden
  • Password manager: offers to save when you sign in, then fills in for you; stored in the device’s secure storage
  • Private tabs: no history is kept
  • Private tab lock: opens with your fingerprint, face, screen lock or Windows Hello
  • Site permissions: asks you first for location, camera and notifications
  • Site info: click the lock in the address bar to see whether the connection is secure, which permissions you gave (reset each one) and to delete the site's cookies Windows

  • Accent colours and ready-made themes
  • Dark / light theme that can follow your system; dark mode for websites

Smart address bar

  • Choice of search engine: Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Yandex, Brave, Ecosia, Startpage
  • Voice search Android

Installs in a minute

Windows

  1. Use “Download for Windows” to get Pusula-Kurulum.exe.
  2. Open it. If Windows shows a warning, choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. No administrator rights needed.
  3. If you like, make Pusula Browser your default browser under Settings → Apps → Default apps.

Android

  1. Open this page on your phone and tap “Download for Android”.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, turn on “Allow from this source” and tap “Install”.
  3. If you like, choose Pusula Browser under Settings → Apps → Default apps → Browser.

How do my PC and phone stay in sync?

You sign in to Pusula Browser with the same OA Labs account on both devices. Your bookmarks, quick links, open tabs and history are saved to your account and reach the other device on their own, and your passwords too if you want. A change on one device shows up on the other when you open the app, or within a few minutes while it’s open.

Do I need an account to use the browser?

No. You only need an account to sync your devices. Every feature works without signing in; your data then stays on that device only.

Do my passwords sync, and is it safe?

If you want. You set a sync passphrase in “Sync settings”; your passwords are encrypted with it on your device (AES-256), and only the encrypted copy reaches our server. The passphrase is never sent anywhere, so nobody, OA Labs included, can see your passwords. Type the same passphrase on the other device and they open there too. If you don’t turn on password sync, your passwords stay on that device.

Can I switch from Chrome?

Yes. Export your bookmarks (.html) and passwords (.csv) from Chrome, Edge or Firefox, then choose “Import” from the menu in Pusula Browser. Everything comes over in one go.

Is it paid? Are there ads?

No. Pusula Browser is completely free; we don’t show ads, and it blocks the ones on websites by default.

Which engine shows the pages?

On Windows, Microsoft Edge’s engine (WebView2); on Android, your phone’s own web engine (Android System WebView). Sites look just as they do in Chrome and Edge.

Why does Windows show a warning?

The app isn’t digitally signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” the first time. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Why isn’t it on Google Play?

For now we distribute it directly from here. The app is always signed with the same OA key, so new versions install over the old one and your data isn’t lost.

How does it update?

When a new version is out, the app lets you know; “Check for updates” in the menu downloads and installs it in one tap. You can also keep all OA Labs apps up to date in one place with OA Labs Center.
