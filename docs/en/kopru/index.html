One QR code.
Then always connected.

No cable, no account. Pair the two devices once; after that they stay connected: directly on the same Wi-Fi, over the internet (still encrypted) when you're away.

  1. Install on bothThe Windows version on your PC, the Android version on your phone.
  2. Scan the QR codeClick "Show QR code" on the PC and scan it with the phone. No camera? Enter the 6-digit code.
  3. SendDrag and drop on the PC, "Pusula Köprü" in the share menu on the phone. That's it.

What can you do?

The small things you do between your phone and PC dozens of times a day, down to one tap.

Files and photos

  • Both ways: PC to phone, phone to PC
  • Drag and drop on the PC (folders too), the share menu on the phone
  • No size limit; several files at once
  • Received files land in Downloads / Pusula Köprü

Shared clipboard

  • What you copy on the PC is ready on your phone
  • "Send clipboard" on the phone goes straight to the PC's clipboard
  • Turn it off with one switch

Text and links

  • Move an address, a note or a password without typing
  • Links open on the phone in Pusula Browser
  • "Open" or "Copy" on the PC

Find your phone

  • "Ring phone" from the PC: rings even on silent
  • Stops by itself after 30 seconds
  • See your phone's battery on the PC

Notification mirror Coming soon

  • Your phone's WhatsApp, SMS and other notifications on your PC
  • Reply from the PC
  • Arrives with the Google Play release: Google doesn't allow notification access for apps installed outside Play

PC remote

  • Pause, skip and change the volume of music and video from your phone
  • Lock your PC from your phone as you walk away
  • Send your latest screenshot to the PC in one tap

Away from home too

  • Works when your phone is on mobile data or another network
  • If the other device is off, links are delivered when it's back
  • Up to 100 MB per file

Always ready

  • Receives in the background when the phone app is closed
  • Sits in the PC's system tray and starts with Windows
  • Notifications for what arrives; tap to open
  • "Send to phone" in Pusula Browser

End-to-end encrypted

  • On the same Wi-Fi, files go device to device
  • Everything is end-to-end encrypted (ChaCha20-Poly1305)
  • When away it passes a server, but the server can't read it
  • No account needed, no data collected

Two-minute setup

Windows

  1. Download Pusula-Kopru-Kurulum.exe with "Download for Windows" and open it. If Windows warns you, click "More info", then "Run anyway". No admin rights needed.
  2. If Windows Firewall asks on first launch, click "Allow". Otherwise your phone can't see the PC.
  3. Click "Show QR code".

Android

  1. Open this page on your phone and tap "Download for Android". Open the downloaded file; if asked, allow installs from this source and tap "Install".
  2. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi as the PC.
  3. Open Pusula Köprü, tap "Scan QR code" and scan the code on the PC.

Questions

Do I need internet?

Not on the same Wi-Fi: files travel directly inside your home or office network, fast and without using mobile data. On different networks (e.g. phone on mobile data) they go over the internet.

Does it work across different networks?

Yes. If the devices aren't on the same Wi-Fi, everything goes through the OA server: files (up to 100 MB), links, clipboard, notifications, "Ring phone". Content is encrypted on your device; the server only carries encrypted bytes and deletes files right after they're received (within 24 hours at most). You can turn off "Connect on other networks too" in Settings.

How secure is it?

When pairing, the two devices verify each other with a one-time secret in the QR code and create a key only they know. Every later message and file chunk is encrypted with that key (X25519, HKDF, ChaCha20-Poly1305). Others on the same network can't see what's sent or inject anything. Even when it goes through the server, nobody, including OA Labs, can read the content.

My phone can't see the PC. What now?

Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi (guest networks can stop devices from seeing each other). If you didn't click "Allow" on the firewall prompt at first launch: Settings → Privacy & security → Windows Security → Firewall → "Allow an app through firewall", and tick Pusula Köprü.

Can the phone receive files when the app is closed?

Yes. Pusula Köprü runs in the background with a quiet "Pusula Köprü is on" notification, and starts again after a reboot. You can turn this off in Settings.

When is the notification mirror coming?

It's ready, but Google Play Protect blocks installing apps from outside the Play Store that ask for notification access. So it's off in the downloadable version for now and will be turned on when Pusula Köprü is on Google Play.

Google Play Protect blocked the install

This shouldn't happen from 0.3.2 on. If you're installing an older version, download the latest from this page. If Play Protect says it doesn't recognise the developer, tap "More details" → "Install anyway".

Is there an iPhone or Mac version?

Not yet. Pusula Köprü is for Windows and Android for now.

Why does Windows show a warning?

The app isn't digitally signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" on first launch. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

How does it update?

The app tells you when a new version is out. The Android version is always signed with the same OA key, so updates install over the old one and your pairings stay.
