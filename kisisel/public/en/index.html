FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Things you may wonder

Does it need the internet?

No. Your data stays on your computer and the licence key is verified on the computer. The till works without internet.

How many computers does a licence cover?

One licence covers one computer (it is tied to the computer’s machine code). If you change computers, write to us and we will move the licence.

Are updates paid?

Bug fixes are always free. New features within the same major version are included. For a big new version, existing customers get a discounted upgrade.

Does it replace a cash register (ÖKC)?

No. The program prints an information receipt with no fiscal value. If you are required to use a cash register, keep using it.

Is my data backed up?

Yes. You set up automatic backup and choose the backup folder.
